01. Introduction — What Is Radium World-Clock Studio?
Radium World-Clock Studio is a luxury, zero-dependency horological widget builder designed for websites, live streams, global teams, and social status creators. It combines smooth 60-FPS analog chronographs (with luminous radium hands, 24-hour dials, and 1/10-second sub-dials) alongside crisp digital time readouts, automatic daylight-saving sync, and live Open / Closed business-hour countdowns—all exportable as a single self-contained .html file.

Brand & Colors: Enter your Dial Brand Name, Headline, and pick a Horology Preset (Radium, Gold, Arctic, Crimson, Ivory) or custom hex colors.
Format & Business Hours: Toggle 12-Hour (AM/PM) or 24-Hour, pick HH:MM / HH:MM:SS / Analog Only, and set your Open/Close times.
Select 1–8 Watches: Pick how many watches you need and click Zone on each card to search and assign cities.
Download & Embed: Click Download Single-File .HTML and upload it to WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, cPanel, or open it directly in any browser.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q: Does the downloaded HTML file require any backend or paid API?
No. It uses your browser's built-in Intl.DateTimeFormat engine, so daylight saving changes and timezone offsets stay accurate forever with zero API keys.
Q: How can I use this on my WhatsApp Status or Facebook DP?
Click Clean Status View to hide the editor sidebar, then take a crisp screenshot for your DP or record a 10–15 second video clip for your WhatsApp Status or Facebook Story.
Q: Can I embed the exported file inside an existing webpage?
Yes! You can either embed it via an <iframe src="your-widget.html"> or copy the <section id="rwSection"> block, <style>, and <script> directly into any page.
