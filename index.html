01. Introduction — What Is Radium World-Clock Studio?

Radium World-Clock Studio is a luxury, zero-dependency horological widget builder designed for websites, live streams, global teams, and social status creators. It combines smooth 60-FPS analog chronographs (with luminous radium hands, 24-hour dials, and 1/10-second sub-dials) alongside crisp digital time readouts, automatic daylight-saving sync, and live Open / Closed business-hour countdowns—all exportable as a single self-contained .html file.

02. Key Features at a Glance

1 to 8 Synchronized Clocks Choose anywhere from 1 hero watch to 8 global cities with automatic responsive grid centering.
Searchable Global Timezones Search 80+ curated cities (Bhopal, New Delhi, London, New York, Dubai, Tokyo) plus every IANA zone.
Open / Close Live Countdowns Set working hours & schedules (Mon–Fri, Mon–Sat, Everyday, 24×7) with real-time "Closes in 2h 15m" badges.
1-Click Standalone Export Download a single .html file with all custom colors, brand names, and CSS baked inline—no external servers needed.

03. Where & Who Can Use It (Use Cases)

  • Websites & Contact Pages: Show visitors your global office hours and live support availability across India, UK, US, UAE, and APAC.
  • WhatsApp Status & Facebook Stories: Click Clean Status View, record a 10-second loop of the sweeping second hand, and share a signature luxury time card with your own brand name on the dial.
  • Live Streams, Webinars & Coaching: Display upcoming session times and use the Meeting Planner slider to check attendee hours worldwide.
  • Agencies & Freelancers: Generate white-labeled clocks with each client's brand name and colors in under 60 seconds.

04. How-To: Customize & Embed in 4 Steps

  1. Brand & Colors: Enter your Dial Brand Name, Headline, and pick a Horology Preset (Radium, Gold, Arctic, Crimson, Ivory) or custom hex colors.
  2. Format & Business Hours: Toggle 12-Hour (AM/PM) or 24-Hour, pick HH:MM / HH:MM:SS / Analog Only, and set your Open/Close times.
  3. Select 1–8 Watches: Pick how many watches you need and click Zone on each card to search and assign cities.
  4. Download & Embed: Click Download Single-File .HTML and upload it to WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, cPanel, or open it directly in any browser.

05. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Does the downloaded HTML file require any backend or paid API? No. It uses your browser's built-in Intl.DateTimeFormat engine, so daylight saving changes and timezone offsets stay accurate forever with zero API keys.
Q: How can I use this on my WhatsApp Status or Facebook DP? Click Clean Status View to hide the editor sidebar, then take a crisp screenshot for your DP or record a 10–15 second video clip for your WhatsApp Status or Facebook Story.
Q: Can I embed the exported file inside an existing webpage? Yes! You can either embed it via an <iframe src="your-widget.html"> or copy the <section id="rwSection"> block, <style>, and <script> directly into any page.
